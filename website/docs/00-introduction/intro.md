# Introduction

## Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

This comprehensive course covers the fundamentals and advanced concepts of Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics.

### Course Overview

The course is structured into multiple parts that build upon each other to provide a complete understanding of Physical AI.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will understand:
- The fundamental principles of Physical AI
- How to design and implement humanoid robotic systems
- ROS 2 concepts and implementation
- Simulation techniques and real-world deployment

### Target Audience

This course is designed for students and professionals with a background in computer science, robotics, or related fields.

## Course Structure

### Part I: Foundations

Foundational concepts that underpin Physical AI and humanoid robotics.

### Part II: Technical Implementation

Technical aspects of building and programming humanoid robots.

### Part III: Advanced Applications

Advanced applications and cutting-edge research in the field.

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of programming concepts
- Familiarity with robotics fundamentals
- Mathematics background (linear algebra, calculus)
