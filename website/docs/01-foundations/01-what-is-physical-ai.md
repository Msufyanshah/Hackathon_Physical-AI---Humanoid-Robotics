# What is Physical AI?

## Introduction to Physical AI

Physical AI represents a significant shift from traditional digital AI systems to embodied intelligence that interacts with the physical world.

### Definition and Core Concepts

Physical AI combines artificial intelligence with physical systems, creating machines that can perceive, reason, and act in the physical environment.

### Historical Context

The development of Physical AI builds on decades of robotics and AI research.

## Key Components

### Perception Systems

Physical AI systems require sophisticated perception capabilities to understand their environment.

### Cognition and Planning

The cognitive layer processes sensory information and makes decisions about actions.

### Actuation and Control

Physical systems must execute actions in the real world with precision and safety.

## Applications

### Industrial Robotics

Physical AI is revolutionizing manufacturing and industrial processes.

### Service Robotics

Robots that assist humans in daily tasks and services.

### Research Platforms

Advanced research platforms for developing and testing Physical AI systems.

## Challenges and Considerations

### Safety and Reliability

Ensuring physical AI systems operate safely in human environments.

### Real-time Processing

The need for rapid decision-making in dynamic environments.

### Embodiment

The importance of physical form in AI systems.
