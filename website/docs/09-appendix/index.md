# Appendix: Supplementary Materials for Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

## Welcome to the Appendix

This appendix contains supplementary materials that support the main course content on Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics. These resources provide additional information, detailed specifications, practical guides, and reference materials to enhance your understanding and implementation of the concepts covered in the main chapters.

## Available Resources

The appendix is organized into the following sections:

- **[Hardware Kits & Components](./hardware-kits.md)**: Detailed information about physical AI hardware platforms
- **[Sim-to-Real Transfer Cookbook](./sim-to-real-cookbook.md)**: Practical approaches for transferring simulation results to real robots 
- **[Troubleshooting Guide](./troubleshooting.md)**: Solutions to common problems in humanoid robotics development
- **[Glossary of Terms](./glossary.md)**: Definitions of key terminology used throughout the course

## How to Use This Appendix

The appendix materials are designed to complement the main course content:

1. **Reference During Projects**: Consult relevant appendix sections while working on practical projects
2. **Hardware Selection**: Use the hardware guide when choosing components for your projects
3. **Problem Solving**: Refer to the troubleshooting guide when encountering implementation challenges
4. **Terminology**: Consult the glossary when encountering unfamiliar terms

## Additional Resources

This appendix will be expanded throughout the course with additional materials including:

- **Safety Guidelines**: Comprehensive safety protocols for humanoid robotics
- **Advanced Implementation Patterns**: Complex implementation strategies
- **Research Papers Index**: Curated list of seminal papers in physical AI
- **Community Resources**: Links to relevant communities and forums
- **Further Reading**: Extended bibliography for continued learning

The appendix serves as a growing repository of practical knowledge and reference materials that will continue to expand as the field of humanoid robotics evolves. We encourage students to contribute to these resources as they develop their own insights and solutions during the course.

As you progress through the course, these supplementary materials will become increasingly valuable as references for practical implementation challenges and advanced concepts that build on the foundational material covered in the main chapters.

## Contribution Guidelines

Students and contributors are encouraged to submit additions and improvements to the appendix materials, particularly:

- Solutions to commonly encountered problems
- Hardware recommendations and reviews
- Sim-to-real transfer experiences
- Additional terminology for the glossary

Please follow the contribution guidelines provided in the course repository when submitting improvements to these materials.
